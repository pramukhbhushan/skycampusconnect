Hackathon Track 5: Cut down volunteers’ finance paperwork

Problem: Volunteers who run events and programs end up doing the finance work around them, like reimbursements, invoices and budget tracking. It’s mostly manual, takes up a lot of their time and wears down their goodwill.

How it works now:

A teacher finishes a retreat.
They upload the receipts to the same folder they used to announce the course.
Someone makes a summary PDF that includes the receipts.
The receipts get pulled out and sent to accounts payable.

The process is different in the US and Canada. That probably means different currencies, tax rules (such as GST/HST) and approval paths.

What to build: A tool that cuts down this manual finance work. For example, it could:

Let volunteers submit expense reimbursements and get them approved
Generate and track invoices

Requirement: You have to build it and demo it using the sample data they provide.

Main pain points to target: duplicate uploads, putting the receipt PDF together by hand, the handoff to accounts payable, and handling the two country setups.
